Why use this converter?

⇄

Latin ↔ Cyrillic

Convert between Latin and Cyrillic where the selected standard supports both directions. One-way standards convert Cyrillic to Latin only; Serbian digraphs such as lj, nj and dž are also supported.

🌍

Many Slavic languages

Serbian, Belarusian, Bulgarian, Macedonian, Montenegrin, Russian and Ukrainian transliteration. Standards include ISO 9, GOST 7.79 System B, GOST R 7.0.34-2014, BGN/PCGN 1947, ICAO Doc 9303, KMU Resolution No. 55, DSTU 9112, the Bulgarian Streamlined System and Belarusian Instructions (2007/2023).

🔒

Free and private

Runs entirely in your browser. Your text is never uploaded to a server.
