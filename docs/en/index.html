Frequently asked questions

Is the Latin to Cyrillic converter free?

Yes. The web version is free to use, and apps for Android and iOS are also available.

Which languages are supported?

Serbian, Russian, Ukrainian, Bulgarian, Belarusian, Macedonian and Montenegrin, including ISO 9:1995 transliteration.

Can I convert Cyrillic back to Latin?

Yes. The converter works in both directions: Latin to Cyrillic and Cyrillic to Latin.

Is my text sent to a server?

No. The conversion happens locally in your browser, so your text stays on your device.

Can I learn the Cyrillic alphabet?

Yes, with the app for Android and iOS. It includes an alphabet overview, flashcards and quizzes for every supported alphabet. The first Letter Set is free; all learning features are unlocked with a one-time in-app purchase.
