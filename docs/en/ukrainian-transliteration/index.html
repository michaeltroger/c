Ukrainian transliteration

Ukrainian uses its own version of the Cyrillic alphabet with letters such as Ґ, Є, І and Ї. The Cyrillic.app converter transliterates Ukrainian text into Latin letters with the international standard ISO 9:1995, where every letter has exactly one Latin counterpart – so the result can be converted back to Cyrillic.

Letters unique to Ukrainian

Unlike Russian, Ukrainian distinguishes И (i) from І (ì), and has Ї (ï), Є (ê) and Ґ (g̀). ISO 9 marks these differences with diacritics, so words like "їжак" (ïžak) or "ґанок" (g̀anok) keep their exact spelling.

The apostrophe

The Ukrainian apostrophe (’) separates sounds, as in "м’ясо". It is not a letter, but it matters for correct spelling, so it is kept unchanged in the transliteration.

Frequently asked questions

Is ISO 9 the same as the passport transliteration?

No. Ukrainian passports use the national system adopted by the Cabinet of Ministers in 2010 (e.g. Ж → zh, Х → kh). ISO 9 is used when an exact, reversible transliteration is needed.

Can I convert Latin text back to Ukrainian?

Yes. Text written in ISO 9 Latin can be converted back to Ukrainian Cyrillic without loss.

Is my text sent to a server?

No. The web converter runs entirely in your browser, and the Android and iOS apps work fully offline.
